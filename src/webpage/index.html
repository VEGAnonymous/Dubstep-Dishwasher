Project Information

We want to make a system that allows users to remotely control the processing of waveforms, specifically for electric guitars/instruments, via a phone app in addition to tinyML implementation that allows users to automatically control their settings without the need of another person. We plan to have a variety of effects that users can visualize and control.

Target Audience

  • Electric guitar fans - These people may be looking to try something new related to the hobby. We believe it would also offer additional convenience by eliminating the need for a full pedalboard.
  • People with interest in AI - These people are usually looking for all sorts of new and creative ways to use AI in our technology.
  • Solo players - AI can allow solo players to have real time sound control without needing an additional person or doing processing on a recording.

Deliverables

At the end of the quarter we plan to showcase do a live showcase with a guitar. This will showcase the processing of the sounds and us controlling the effects using the app. Ideally we would also like to show the tinyML implementation where the audience can see the effects being changed in real time.

Critical Features

  • A functioning DSP circuit with a wide variety of effects such as
    • reverb
    • delay
    • compressor
    • limiter
    • distortion
    • chorus
    • flanger
    • saturation
  • A functioning app that allows users to control effects and view waveforms remotely
  • A basic tinyML implemenation that can automatically control effects. We do not expect it to sound good are work well we just expect it to work.

Performance Metrics

  • <20-50ms of delay for controls via bluetooth app
  • <20ms of delay for digital signal processing (still TBD)
  • Effects listed above
  • <200ms of delay for tinyML (still TBD)

Goals

  • Our 1st goal is to have a controllable system that does all the audio processing using ADC (analog to digital), DAC (digital to analog), and DSP (digital signal processing) circuits. As the rest of the project does not function without this, this is our 1st goal
  • Our 2nd goal is to create an android-based phone app that offers the capabilities of a pedalboard with the convenience of a smartphone using bluetooth technology.
  • Our finals goal are the additional features such as tinyML implementation allowing for automatic controls and wavetable synthesis to allow for a wider variety of sounds.

Risks/Constraints

  • AI requires training and it is unlikely that it will apply effects in a way that sounds pleasing to users with minimal training. If possible we will try to use libraries to ease implementation and existing training data.
  • Using bluetooth communication to control the effects will result in undesired latency which we will need to minimize as much as possible
  • None of us are front-end or app developers so app development may be difficult. To try and alleviate this we will split up responsibilities based on what everyone has the most expertise in.
